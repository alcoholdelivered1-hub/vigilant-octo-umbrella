## Description
Alcohol Delivered connects customers with licensed Arkansas alcohol retailers for local delivery. Browse participating stores, place an order, complete required age and ID verification, and track delivery status.

Key features:
- Browse participating local retailers and products
- Secure online checkout
- Age and identity verification
- Delivery tracking and order status updates
- Driver assignment and delivery coordination
- Customer order history and ratings
- Retailer inventory and order management
- Compliance-focused delivery workflows

Alcohol Delivered is a technology and logistics platform. Licensed retailers remain the sellers of record for alcohol transactions. Availability, delivery areas, hours, and eligibility are subject to applicable Arkansas requirements and participating retailer policies.
